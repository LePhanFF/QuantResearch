Decision Playbook

Phase 1 -- When to Enter

  • SELL PUT when IVR >= 50 at 0.20 delta, IVR 30-50 at 0.25 delta
  • BUY STOCK when IVR < 20, or strong breakout, or div capture
  • STAND ASIDE if earnings in DTE, or >10% below 200 SMA

Phase 2 -- Put Management

  • 50% profit hit: Close, re-sell 30-45 DTE
  • OTM, > 21 DTE: Hold
  • OTM, <= 21 DTE: Roll same strike +30d
  • ITM < 3%: Roll same strike +30d for credit
  • ITM 3-8%: Roll down 1-2 strikes +45d (credit only!)
  • ITM > 8%: Accept assignment

Phase 3 -- Covered Calls After Assignment

  • > 5% above cost basis: 0.30-0.40 delta (get called away)
  • Near cost basis: 0.25 delta (standard)
  • 2-10% below basis: 0.15-0.20 delta (patient)
  • > 10% below: 0.10-0.15 delta (wait for recovery)
  • Never sell below cost basis

Phase 4 -- Call Management

  • 50% profit: Close, re-sell
  • ITM <= 7 DTE, above basis: Let call away (cycle complete!)
  • ITM <= 7 DTE, below basis: Roll up and out
  • Stock fell > 8%: Roll call down (never below basis)

When to Buy Stock Outright

  • IV Rank < 20 -- premium too thin for CSP
  • Strong breakout -- don't miss the move with a CSP
  • Dividend capture -- ex-div before CSP expiry + low IV
  • Core holding you never want called away
  • Price at support in uptrend + low IV

Concentration Limits

  • Max 20% of account in any single underlying
  • Max 35% in any single sector
  • Keep 25% cash buffer for assignments
  • DTE sweet spot: 30-45 days
