ENTRY RULES

IVR >= 50 → SELL PUT at 0.20 delta
IVR 30-50 → SELL PUT at 0.25 delta
IVR < 20 → BUY STOCK outright
RSI < 30 + cheap P/E + above 200 SMA → BUY (prime dip)
10-25% off 52w high + above 200 SMA → pullback zone
RSI > 70 + P/E > 40 → STAND ASIDE (overbought + expensive)
>10% below 200 SMA → STAND ASIDE (falling knife)
Earnings in DTE window → STAND ASIDE

PUT MANAGEMENT

50% profit → CLOSE, re-sell 30-45 DTE
OTM, > 21 DTE → HOLD
ITM < 3% → ROLL same strike +30d (credit)
ITM 3-8% → ROLL DOWN 1-2 strikes +45d
ITM > 8% → ACCEPT ASSIGNMENT
NEVER roll for a debit

COVERED CALLS

> 5% above basis → 0.30-0.40 delta (get called)
Near basis → 0.25 delta (standard)
2-10% below → 0.15-0.20 delta (patient)
> 10% below → 0.10-0.15 delta (wait)
Never sell below adjusted cost basis

CALL MANAGEMENT

50% profit → Close, re-sell
ITM <= 7 DTE, above basis → LET CALL AWAY
ITM <= 7 DTE, below basis → Roll up and out
Stock fell > 8% → Roll call down (above basis)

WHEN TO BUY OUTRIGHT

IV Rank < 20 (premium too thin)
RSI < 30 (oversold) + good P/E
10-25% pullback from 52w high
Strong breakout + low IV
Core holding you never want called away

RISK LIMITS

Max 20% per ticker
Max 35% per sector
Keep 25% cash buffer
DTE sweet spot: 30-45 days
Poor R/R: Ann ROC < 50% of 6w drawdown
