## What Is IGETC?

**IGETC (Intersegmental General Education Transfer Curriculum)** is a standardized
set of general education courses that satisfies the lower-division GE requirements
at **every UC and CSU campus**. It is the single most powerful tool for dual
enrollment students because completing it at a community college means Aaron
walks into his university with **zero general education left to take**.

Without IGETC, a UC student typically spends **3-5 quarters** (or 2-3 semesters
at Berkeley/Merced) on general education before focusing on their major. That's
roughly **1 to 1.5 years** of college dedicated to breadth courses. IGETC
eliminates all of that.

## What IGETC Covers

IGETC spans 6 broad areas designed to give students a well-rounded foundation
across writing, math, arts, humanities, social sciences, and natural sciences:

| Area | What It Covers | Units | GWC Course |
|------|---------------|-------|--------------------|
| **1A** | English Composition -- college-level writing | 3 | ENGL G100 |
| **1B** | Critical Thinking & Composition -- argument and analysis | 3 | ENGL G110 |
| **1C** | Oral Communication (CSU only) -- public speaking | 3 | SPCH G100 |
| **2** | Math / Quantitative Reasoning -- statistics or calculus | 3-4 | MATH G160 (Stats) or MATH G180 (Calc) |
| **3A** | Arts -- appreciation of visual/performing arts | 3 | ART G100, MUSC G100, or THEA G100 |
| **3B** | Humanities -- philosophy, literature, or history of ideas | 3 | PHIL G100, HUM G100, or a Literature course |
| **4** | Social & Behavioral Sciences -- 3 courses from 2+ disciplines | 9 | PSYC G100 + SOC G100 + HIST G100 |
| **5A** | Physical Science -- chemistry, physics, or earth science | 3-5 | CHEM G180 (pre-med) or PHYS G170 |
| **5B** | Biological Science -- biology with lab component | 3-4 | BIOL G100 or BIOL G110 (majors) |
| **5C** | Lab requirement -- at least one of 5A/5B must include a lab | -- | Included in CHEM G180 / BIOL courses |
| **6** | Language Other Than English | -- | 2 yrs same language in HS (C or better) |

**Total: ~34-37 semester units**

## Why IGETC Matters

**Benefits for Aaron:**

- **Graduate early** -- finish a bachelor's degree in 3 years instead of 4, or
  use the extra time for a Master's program, research, or clinical experience
- **Lighter university course load** -- take fewer classes per quarter/semester,
  leaving time for research, internships, and extracurriculars
- **Focus on the major** -- dive into upper-division pre-med or STEM courses
  from day one at a UC
- **Stronger med school application** -- more time for research hours, clinical
  volunteering, and MCAT prep
- **Accepted systemwide** -- IGETC is honored at all 9 UC campuses and all 23
  CSU campuses, so Aaron isn't locked into one school

## How Much Money Does IGETC Save?

The cost difference between completing GE at a community college vs. a UC is
dramatic:

| | Community College (GWC) | UC (as a freshman) |
|--|------------------------|-------------------|
| **Tuition for ~35 units of GE** | **Free** (CA concurrent enrollment fee waiver) | ~$15,000-$18,000 (flat-rate quarterly tuition for ~3-4 quarters) |
| **Room & board** (if living on/near campus) | $0 (lives at home) | ~$18,000-$22,000/year |
| **Textbooks & fees** | ~$200-$400 total | ~$1,000-$2,000 |

### Savings Breakdown

| Scenario | Estimated Savings |
|----------|------------------|
| **Tuition only** (GE units at CC vs UC) | **~$15,000-$18,000** |
| **Graduate 1 year early** (skip a full year of UC) | **~$35,000-$40,000** (tuition + room & board) |
| **Graduate 1.5-2 years early** (Option B or C) | **~$55,000-$75,000** |
| **Opportunity cost** (enter workforce or grad school 1-2 years sooner) | Potentially **$50,000-$150,000+** in lifetime earnings |

> **Bottom line:** Completing IGETC at GWC for essentially free saves Aaron a
> minimum of **$15,000 in tuition** and up to **$75,000+ in total college costs**
> depending on how many years of university he skips. For a pre-med student,
> graduating early also means starting residency sooner -- a massive financial
> and career advantage.

### UC Annual Cost of Attendance (2025-2026 estimates, CA residents)

## AP + CC Synergies

### How AP Credit Works with IGETC

AP exam scores of **3 or higher** can satisfy specific IGETC areas at UC and CSU.
This is real, official credit -- not just placement. However, there are important
nuances:

- **UC accepts AP scores of 3+** for most IGETC areas (some competitive majors
  want 4+)
- **CSU accepts AP scores of 3+** across the board
- AP credit is awarded **after** the exam (scores arrive July), so Aaron can't
  use AP credit to satisfy a CC prerequisite in real-time
- **CC courses are more reliable** for IGETC certification -- AP credit depends
  on the score, while a CC grade of C or better is guaranteed to count
- **Maximum of 2 AP exams** can apply to a single IGETC area

### The Overlap Problem: How to Avoid Doubling Up

Since Aaron plans to take **8-13 AP classes**, there will be significant overlap
with CC courses. The strategy is simple: **plan CC courses first, let AP scores
be the bonus**.

**Why CC first, AP second:**
1. CC courses are taken **before** AP exams -- results are guaranteed (grade vs
   hoping for a 3+)
2. If Aaron takes a CC course AND scores 3+ on the equivalent AP, the extra
   credit still counts as **elective units** at UC (not wasted)
3. The CC course helps Aaron **ace the AP exam** -- double preparation

**However, for courses Aaron hasn't taken at CC yet**, a strong AP score can
**replace the need** to take it at CC, freeing that slot.

### Recommended Strategy by AP Class

| AP at FVHS | CC Equivalent | IGETC Area | Strategy |
|------------|--------------|------------|----------|
| AP English Language | ENGL G100 | 1A | **Take CC first (Summer before 9th).** CC is more reliable for IGETC. AP score is bonus credit. |
| AP Psychology | PSYC G100 | 4 | **Skip CC Psych if taking AP.** Score of 3+ satisfies Area 4. Freed CC slot for coding or rest. |
| AP US History | HIST G100 | 4 | **Skip CC History if taking AP.** Score of 3+ satisfies Area 4. Freed CC slot. |
| AP Statistics | MATH G160 | 2 | **Take CC Stats first (Summer before 9th).** If Aaron later takes AP Stats, the AP score adds elective credit. Or: skip CC Stats and take Calculus (MATH G180) instead. |
| AP Biology | BIOL G100/G110 | 5B | **Take AP at FVHS for the learning; still take CC Bio for guaranteed IGETC lab credit.** AP Bio often doesn't include the lab component CC requires. |
| AP Chemistry | CHEM G180 | 5A | **Take CC Chem for official IGETC credit + lab.** AP Chem is great prep but CC gives the guaranteed transcript. |
| AP Calculus AB/BC | MATH G180 | 2 | **If taking AP Calc, skip CC Calc.** Score of 3+ satisfies math. Use freed summer slot for a science. |
| AP World/Euro History | -- | 3B or 4 | **Skip CC Humanities/History if taking this AP.** Freed CC slot. |
| AP Macroeconomics | ECON G170 | 4 | **Skip CC Econ if taking AP.** One fewer online class needed. |
| AP Government | -- | 4 | **Skip CC Sociology if taking AP Gov.** Both satisfy Area 4. |

### With 8-13 APs: How the Plans Simplify

If Aaron takes 8-13 AP classes across high school, several IGETC areas get
covered by AP scores alone. Here's how the CC course load drops:

**AP classes most likely to clear IGETC areas** (assuming scores of 3+):

| IGETC Area | Cleared By AP | CC Course No Longer Needed |
|------------|--------------|---------------------------|
| 4 (Social Sciences, need 3) | AP Psych + AP US History + AP Gov/Econ | PSYC G100, HIST G100, SOC G100 -- **save 9 units** |
| 3B (Humanities) | AP World or Euro History | PHIL G100 -- **save 3 units** |
| 3A (Arts) | AP Art History or Music Theory | ART G100 -- **save 3 units** |
| 2 (Math) | AP Calc AB/BC | Can take Calc at CC for pre-med, or skip if AP covers it |

**Potential savings: 9-15 fewer CC units needed**, meaning:
- **Option A** could complete IGETC in just 3 summers instead of 5
- **Option B** could drop to 2-4 online classes total instead of 6
- **Option C** replaces cleared GE slots with more pre-med prerequisites

> **Key insight:** With 8-13 AP classes, Aaron likely covers 3-5 IGETC areas
> through AP alone. The freed CC slots can go toward **pre-med prerequisites**
> (Gen Chem II, Gen Bio I & II, Calculus), **coding courses**, or simply a
> **lighter schedule**. The combination of AP + CC is more powerful than either
> alone -- AP classes build knowledge and impress admissions, while CC courses
> guarantee the IGETC transcript credit.

### Important: Don't Skip CC Courses You've Already Registered For

If Aaron is already enrolled in a CC course and later takes the equivalent AP:
- **Finish the CC course** -- the grade is on his transcript either way
- The AP score adds **bonus elective credit** at UC (not wasted)
- Withdrawing mid-course shows a "W" on the CC transcript (looks bad)

## Optional Pre-Med Add-Ons

If Aaron completes IGETC early (especially with Option B or C), these courses
build the pre-med foundation. Med schools accept intro-level sciences from CC.

| Course | Units | Why | Best Taken |
|--------|-------|-----|------------|
| MATH G180 - Calculus I | 4 | Required by most med schools | Summer (in-person) |
| CHEM G185 - Gen Chem II + Lab | 5 | Continues pre-med sequence | Summer or online |
| BIOL G110 - General Biology I (majors) | 5 | Stronger than BIOL G100 for pre-med | Summer (in-person) |
| BIOL G115 - General Biology II + Lab | 5 | Completes bio sequence | Online or summer |
| CS G120 / OCC CS A155 - Python | 3 | Bioinformatics, research data skills | Summer or online |
| ECON G170 - Macroeconomics | 3 | Broadens knowledge, Area 4 alt | Online |

### What to Save for UC (Don't Take at CC)

Med schools prefer these taken at a 4-year university:
- Organic Chemistry I & II
- Biochemistry
- Physics I & II
- Upper-division biology courses

**Rule of thumb:** Only add extras if GPA is 3.8+ at both HS and CC.
