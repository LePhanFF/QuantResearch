# Aaron's Dual Enrollment Plan
## Fountain Valley High School + Golden West College / Orange Coast College

**Goal:** Complete UC/CSU transferable general education courses during high school
via dual enrollment, so Aaron can graduate college early or pursue a Master's
degree in years 3-4 at a UC.

**Starting Point:** Aaron finishes 8th grade May 2026 and begins dual enrollment
the summer before 9th grade, giving him **4 full summers** before HS graduation.

**Interests:** Pre-Med / Medical Field, Coding, STEM, Speech

**Philosophy:** High school comes first -- AP classes, teacher relationships for
recommendation letters, and extracurriculars are the priority. Community college
is the **supplement**, not the main course.

## Which Option to Pick

| If Aaron's situation is... | Choose |
|---------------------------|--------|
| Heavy AP load (4+ AP classes/year), needs max HS focus | **Option A** |
| Moderate AP load, can handle ~4 hrs/wk extra | **Option B** (recommended) |
| Lighter AP load or very driven, wants max college head start | **Option C** |
| Unsure | **Start with B, drop to A if needed** |

### The "Start B, Fall Back to A" Strategy

Start each semester planning to take 1 online class (Option B). If any semester
gets too heavy:
- Simply **skip the online class** that term
- It naturally falls back to Option A pacing
- Nothing is lost -- the course moves to a later semester or summer
- No permanent commitment to either pace
- Starting before 9th grade gives **extra buffer** -- even dropping semesters, Aaron
  stays ahead of the original plan

### The "Upgrade to C" Path

If Aaron breezes through 9th grade with Option B:
- Add a second online class starting 10th grade
- This naturally upgrades to Option C pacing
- Can downgrade back to B at any time

**Flexibility is the whole point.** These aren't rigid tracks -- they're guidelines.
Starting a year early makes every option more forgiving.

## What Is IGETC?

**IGETC (Intersegmental General Education Transfer Curriculum)** is a standardized
package of ~34-37 units of general education that is accepted at **all 9 UC
campuses and all 23 CSU campuses**. Completing IGETC at a community college
means Aaron enters university with **zero general education left** -- he can
jump straight into his major from day one.

It covers 6 areas: English, Math, Arts & Humanities, Social Sciences, Natural
Sciences, and Foreign Language. A typical UC student spends 1-1.5 years on these
courses. By finishing them at GWC for free, Aaron saves **$15,000-$75,000+** in
UC tuition and living costs depending on how many years he shaves off.

**For full details -- requirements, course lists, cost breakdowns, and how AP
classes overlap with IGETC -- see the [IGETC Guide](IGETC_Guide.md).**

## Optional Pre-Med Add-Ons

If Aaron completes IGETC early (especially with Option B or C), these courses
build the pre-med foundation. Med schools accept intro-level sciences from CC.
See also the [IGETC Guide](IGETC_Guide.md) for how AP classes can free up CC
slots for these extras.

| Course | Units | Why | Best Taken |
|--------|-------|-----|------------|
| MATH G180 - Calculus I | 4 | Required by most med schools | Summer (in-person) |
| CHEM G185 - Gen Chem II + Lab | 5 | Continues pre-med sequence | Summer or online |
| BIOL G110 - General Biology I (majors) | 5 | Stronger than BIOL G100 for pre-med | Summer (in-person) |
| BIOL G115 - General Biology II + Lab | 5 | Completes bio sequence | Online or summer |
| CS G120 / OCC CS A155 - Python | 3 | Bioinformatics, research data skills | Summer or online |
| ECON G170 - Macroeconomics | 3 | Broadens knowledge, Area 4 alt | Online |

### What to Save for UC (Don't Take at CC)

Med schools prefer these taken at a 4-year university:
- Organic Chemistry I & II
- Biochemistry
- Physics I & II
- Upper-division biology courses

**Rule of thumb:** Only add extras if GPA is 3.8+ at both HS and CC.

## Costs & Logistics

### Tuition
**Free.** California Education Code waives enrollment fees for concurrently
enrolled high school students at both GWC and OCC.

### Minor Fees

| Fee | Cost | Notes |
|-----|------|-------|
| Health fee | ~$20-25/semester | May be waivable for minors |
| Student rep fee | ~$1-2/semester | Optional |
| Parking permit | ~$30-50/semester | Only for in-person summers |
| Textbooks | $50-200/course | Look for ZTC (Zero Textbook Cost) sections |
| Lab materials | $0-30/course | Some science labs only |

### Total Estimated Cost

| Approach | 3-Year Total |
|----------|-------------|
| Frugal (ZTC sections, no parking, waivers) | **~$200-400** |
| Conservative (all fees, buy textbooks) | **~$800-1,200** |

*Compared to ~$15,000-$50,000+ for the same units at a UC.*

### Enrollment Steps
1. Apply to GWC at **CCCApply** (free, online) -- can apply spring of 8th grade
2. Complete **guided self-placement** for English and math assessment
3. Get **concurrent enrollment form** signed by parent + FVHS counselor (each semester)
   - For Summer 2026: contact FVHS in spring to get the form signed as an incoming 9th grader
4. Register early -- CC classes fill fast, especially summer
5. Verify every course on **ASSIST.org** for your target UC/CSU
6. Request **IGETC certification** from CC counselor before transferring

### Tips
- **GPA is king** -- a 4.0 at CC strengthens UC apps; a B looks mediocre
- **Asynchronous online** = most flexible (no set meeting times)
- **Don't overload junior year** -- most critical year for UC admissions
- **Keep all transcripts** -- need official CC transcripts for UC/CSU applications
- Community college GPA and university GPA are reported **separately** to med schools

### Language Requirement (IGETC Area 6)
- Satisfied by **2 years of same foreign language in HS** with C or better
- AP Spanish/French/etc. also qualifies
- No CC course needed if HS language requirement is met
