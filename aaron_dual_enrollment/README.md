## Important Notes & Tips

### Registration & Logistics
1. **Apply to GWC** as a non-degree-seeking student (free, online application at CCCApply)
2. **Get concurrent enrollment form** signed by parent + FVHS counselor each semester
3. **Register early** - community college classes fill fast, especially summer
4. **Use ASSIST.org** to verify every course transfers to your target UC/CSU campus
5. **Coast CCD concurrent enrollment is tuition-free** for CA high school students (may have small health/materials fees ~$20-50)

### Academic Strategy
- **GPA matters enormously** - only take what Aaron can get A's in
- **Online asynchronous courses** offer most flexibility during the school year
- **Take majors-level science courses** (not survey courses) for pre-med credit
- **Keep all transcripts** - will need official CC transcripts for UC/CSU applications
- **IGETC certification** must be requested from the CC counselor before transfer

### Pre-Med Specific Advice
- Med schools prefer upper-division sciences (Organic Chem, Biochem, Physics) taken at a **4-year university** - save those for UC
- Gen Chem, Gen Bio, Stats, Calculus, Psych, and Sociology at CC are perfectly fine
- MCAT now heavily tests **Psychology and Sociology** - getting these done early is a huge advantage

### Language Requirement (IGETC Area 6)
- Can be satisfied by completing **2 years of the same foreign language in high school** with C or better
- If Aaron takes AP Spanish, French, etc., this is automatically met
- Alternatively, take a college-level language course at GWC/OCC
