# Aaron's Dual Enrollment Plan
## Fountain Valley High School + Golden West College / Orange Coast College

**Goal:** Complete UC/CSU transferable general education courses during high school
via dual enrollment, so Aaron can graduate college early or pursue a Master's
degree in years 3-4 at a UC.

**Interests:** Pre-Med / Medical Field, Coding, STEM, Speech

**Philosophy:** High school comes first -- AP classes, teacher relationships for
recommendation letters, and extracurriculars are the priority during the school
year. Community college is the **supplement**, not the main course. Summers do
the heavy lifting; school year adds just **one easy online class** per semester.

## Guiding Principles

1. **AP classes & teacher recs come first** -- strong letters from HS teachers are
   essential for UC admissions. Don't let CC work interfere.
2. **Summers are the workhorse** -- no HS conflicts, full focus on CC courses
3. **1 online class per semester max** during school year -- asynchronous, low stress
4. **Protect the GPA** -- only take what Aaron can get A's in at both HS and CC
5. **Pick the easiest GE courses for school year** -- save harder courses for summer

## Year-by-Year Plan

---

### Summer Before 10th Grade (Year 1 - Summer)
**In-Person at GWC | 7 units | Full focus, no HS conflicts**

| Course | Units | IGETC Area | Notes |
|--------|-------|------------|-------|
| ENGL G100 - College Composition | 3 | 1A | Gateway -- unlocks ENGL G110 later |
| MATH G160 - Intro to Statistics | 4 | 2 | Med school req; satisfies IGETC math |

> **Why these first?** English Comp is prerequisite for Critical Thinking.
> Statistics is universally required and one of the easier math options.
> Both are offered in 6-8 week summer sessions -- manageable for a motivated student.

---

### 10th Grade - Fall Semester
**1 Online Class | 3 units | Light load alongside AP classes**

| Course | Units | IGETC Area | Notes |
|--------|-------|------------|-------|
| SPCH G100 - Public Speaking | 3 | 1C | Aligns with Aaron's speech interest |

> **Why Speech?** It's engaging, directly relevant to his interests, and online
> sections are often asynchronous with recorded speech submissions. Low homework
> load -- won't compete with AP coursework.

**Aaron's HS focus this semester:** AP classes, extracurriculars, building
teacher relationships for future rec letters.

---

### 10th Grade - Spring Semester
**1 Online Class | 3 units | Light load**

| Course | Units | IGETC Area | Notes |
|--------|-------|------------|-------|

> **Why Art/Music Appreciation?** Minimal time commitment, usually just

> while Aaron focuses on HS finals and AP exams in spring.

**Cumulative after 10th grade: ~13 units, 4 IGETC areas done**

**In-Person at GWC | 8 units | Push harder this summer**

## Important Notes & Tips

### Registration & Logistics
1. **Apply to GWC** as a non-degree student (free, online at CCCApply)
2. **Concurrent enrollment form** signed by parent + FVHS counselor each semester
3. **Register early** -- CC classes fill fast, especially summer
4. **Use ASSIST.org** to verify every course transfers to target UC/CSU
5. **Tuition is free** for CA HS concurrent enrollment (minor fees ~$20-50/semester)

### Academic Strategy
- **GPA is king** -- a 4.0 at CC looks great on UC apps; a B looks mediocre
- **Asynchronous online** = most flexible (no set meeting times)
- **Don't overload junior year** -- it's the most critical year for UC admissions
- **Keep transcripts** -- need official CC transcripts for UC/CSU applications
- **Request IGETC certification** from CC counselor before transferring

### Pre-Med Notes
- Med schools prefer Organic Chem, Biochem, Physics at a **4-year university**
- Gen Chem, Gen Bio, Stats, Psych, Sociology at CC are perfectly fine
- MCAT heavily tests **Psychology and Sociology** -- getting these done early helps
- The community college GPA and university GPA are reported **separately** to med schools

### Language Requirement (IGETC Area 6)
- Satisfied by **2 years of same foreign language in HS** with C or better
- AP Spanish/French/etc. also satisfies this
- No CC course needed if HS language requirement is met
