# Aaron's Dual Enrollment -- Plan Alternatives
## Fountain Valley High School + Golden West College / Orange Coast College

Two pacing options so Aaron can focus on AP classes, teacher recs, and
extracurriculars at FVHS while still knocking out UC/CSU general education.

---

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| | Option A: Summer Only | Option B: Summer + 1 Online/Term |
|--|----------------------|----------------------------------|
| School year CC load | **None** | 1 online class (~3-5 hrs/wk) |
| Summer CC load | 2 courses in-person | 2 courses in-person |
| Years to complete IGETC | 4 summers (before 10th-13th*) | 3 years (done by spring 12th) |
| Total units by HS graduation | **~22-29 units** (partial IGETC) | **~36 units** (full IGETC) |
| IGETC certified before college? | No -- finish remaining at UC/CSU | **Yes -- fully certified** |
| HS stress level | **Lowest** | Low |
| Best for | Max HS focus; finish GE in fall of freshman yr at UC | Full IGETC done before college |

*Option A can use the summer after 12th grade (before starting college) as a 4th summer.

# Head-to-Head Decision Guide

| Factor | Option A (Summer Only) | Option B (Summer + Online) |
|--------|----------------------|---------------------------|
| HS stress | **Lowest possible** | Very low (1 easy online class) |
| IGETC done before college? | No (~80%) | **Yes (100%)** |
| Time saved at UC | ~1 semester of GE remains | **~Full year of GE eliminated** |
| Total CC courses | 8-10 over 4 summers | 13 over 3 yrs |
| Flexibility | Can add online later if easy | Can drop online if HS gets tough |
| Best if... | Aaron's AP/HS load is very heavy | Aaron can handle ~4 hrs/wk extra |

### Recommendation

**Start with Option B** (summer + 1 online/term). If any semester feels too
heavy with AP classes, simply **skip the online class that term** -- it naturally
falls back to Option A pacing. Nothing is lost; the course just moves to a
later semester or summer.

This "start B, fall back to A" approach gives Aaron:
- Maximum flexibility
- No permanent commitment to either pace
- The **option** to finish IGETC early without the **obligation**
