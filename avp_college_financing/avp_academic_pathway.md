# AVP Academic Pathway — Political Science, Law School Prep & Study Abroad

**Student:** AVP — UC Riverside, Fall 2026 Entry
**Goal:** Build an exceptional profile for **law school admission**, **government internships**, and **policy careers** through strategic academics, study abroad (Spain/EU), leadership, and experiential learning.

---

## 1. Recommended Majors & Minors

### Pathway A: Political Science (Public Law) + Public Policy Minor (Strongest for Law School)

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| **Major** | B.A. Political Science — **Public Law concentration** |
| **Minor** | Public Policy (UCR School of Public Policy) |
| **Why** | Public Law concentration is designed for pre-law; policy minor adds quantitative/analytical depth that law schools value |
| **Differentiator** | Combines constitutional/legal theory with real-world policy analysis — signals serious intellectual preparation |

### Pathway B: Political Science (International Relations) + Global Studies Minor (Strongest for Abroad + Govt)

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| **Major** | B.A. Political Science — **International Relations concentration** |
| **Minor** | Global Studies |
| **Why** | Directly supports EU study abroad semester; opens doors to State Dept., international orgs, foreign service |
| **Differentiator** | Study abroad in Madrid/EU becomes a core academic experience, not just a side trip |

### Pathway C: Public Policy Major + Political Science Minor (Most Unique for Law School)

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| **Major** | B.A. Public Policy (UCR School of Public Policy) |
| **Minor** | Political Science |
| **Why** | Public Policy is a newer, more distinctive major — fewer applicants have it. Combines economics, statistics, politics. |
| **Differentiator** | Law schools increasingly value quantitative/analytical skills. This major shows you can do data AND law. Stands out vs. thousands of PoliSci applicants. |

### Pathway D: Double Major — Political Science + Public Policy (Maximum Depth)

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| **Double Major** | B.A. Political Science (Public Law) + B.A. Public Policy |
| **Why** | If course overlap allows it (likely ~4 shared courses), this gives the strongest possible academic profile |
| **Risk** | Course load may be heavy; must maintain high GPA (3.7+ target for T14 law schools) |
| **Differentiator** | Very few undergrads have both. Signals serious commitment + breadth. |

## 4. Study Abroad — Madrid & EU Deep Dive

### Why Madrid for a Future Lawyer

| Factor | Value |
|---|---|
| **Civil law vs. common law** | Spain uses civil law (code-based); understanding both systems is rare among US law applicants |
| **EU law exposure** | Proximity to Brussels; coursework in EU institutions and regulations |
| **Spanish fluency** | Bilingual lawyers are in high demand; immigration law, international practice |
| **International Human Rights** | Spain's post-Franco democratic transition is a rich case study |
| **Personal statement material** | "Studying comparative constitutional law in Madrid transformed my understanding of legal systems" |

### Recommended UCEAP Madrid Courses

| Course | How It Helps |
|---|---|
| European Union: Politics & Policy | Understand supranational governance |
| Spanish Constitutional Law | Comparative law analysis |
| International Human Rights Law | Public interest law foundation |
| Migration & Refugee Policy in Europe | Timely policy issue; connects to US immigration law |
| Spanish Language (Intensive) | Build professional-level fluency |
| European Economic Policy | Policy minor elective credit |

### EU Policy Tour (During Fall Break / Long Weekends)

| Week | Destination | Key Visits |
|---|---|---|
| Week 4 break | **Brussels** | EU Parliament, European Commission, NATO HQ |
| Week 7 weekend | **The Hague** | International Court of Justice, International Criminal Court |
| Week 10 break | **Strasbourg** | European Court of Human Rights; Council of Europe |
| Optional | **Geneva** | UN Geneva (UNHCR, WHO, WTO); Red Cross Museum |

**Budget these trips into the 529 plan** — travel for academic enrichment during study abroad is a legitimate educational expense. Flights within Europe are $30–80 on budget carriers.

## 8. Faculty Relationship Strategy

Law school requires **3 strong letters of recommendation** (2 academic, 1 professional). Build these strategically:

| Relationship | How | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **PoliSci Professor #1** | Take 2+ courses; attend office hours; become research assistant | Y1–Y2 |
| **PoliSci Professor #2** | Honors thesis advisor | Y3–Y4 |
| **Public Policy Professor** | Engage deeply in policy minor courses | Y2–Y3 |
| **DC Internship Supervisor** | Perform exceptionally during UCDC quarter | Y3 |
| **Mock Trial Faculty Advisor** | 4 years of involvement; leadership visible | Y1–Y4 |

**Target:** By Y4, have **5 potential recommenders** so you can choose the strongest 3.
