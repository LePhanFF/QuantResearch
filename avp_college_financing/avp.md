## 5. Scenario Models

### Scenario 1: Maximize Free Money First (Recommended)

**Logic:** Use 529 + Treasury interest to cover as much as possible. Take Stafford for credit-building. Use life insurance only to fill small gaps.

| Year | 529 | Treasury Interest | Stafford | Life Ins. Loan | PLUS | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-27 | $14,200 | $27,300 | $5,500 | $0 | $0 | $47,000 |
| 2027-28 | $14,610 | $27,300 | $6,500 | $0 | $0 | $48,410 |
| 2028-29 | $15,062 | $27,300 | $7,500 | $0 | $0 | $49,862 |
| 2029-30 | $16,558 | $27,300 | $7,500 | $0 | $0 | $51,358 |
| **Total** | **$60,430** | **$109,200** | **$27,000** | **$0** | **$0** | **$196,630** |

**Outcome:**
- 529 remaining: **$24,570** (available for AVLP or Roth IRA rollover)
- Life insurance: **untouched** ($200,000 intact)
- PLUS loans: **$0**
- Student debt: **$27,000** (~$300/mo standard repayment)
- Brokerage principal: **$1,000,000 fully preserved**

**Total cost of borrowing over 10-yr Stafford repayment:** ~$9,800 in interest

**Logic:** Why pay 6.5% to the government when you can borrow from yourself at 0%? Use life insurance in place of Stafford loans.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2027-28 | $14,610 | $27,300 | $0 | $6,500 | $0 | $48,410 |
| 2028-29 | $15,062 | $27,300 | $0 | $7,500 | $0 | $49,862 |
| 2029-30 | $16,558 | $27,300 | $0 | $7,500 | $0 | $51,358 |
| **Total** | **$60,430** | **$109,200** | **$0** | **$27,000** | **$0** | **$196,630** |

**Trade-off:** $27K less death benefit coverage. If that's acceptable, this is strictly cheaper.

## 7. Life Insurance Loan — Repayment Strategy

Since the life insurance loan is at 0%, there's no urgency to repay. However, to restore the full death benefit:

| Repayment Source | Annual Capacity | Time to Repay $60,430 (worst case) |
|---|---|---|
| Treasury interest (post-graduation) | $27,300/yr | ~2.2 years |
| Treasury interest (partial, during school) | $10,000/yr | Offsets during school, remainder in ~1.5 yr post |

**Recommendation:** Make small repayments during school years from excess Treasury interest, then accelerate post-graduation. The 0% rate means there's no mathematical penalty for slow repayment — only the death benefit reduction to consider.

---

## 8. Recommendation

**Scenario 1** is the recommended baseline — it's simple, preserves all major assets, keeps the life insurance fully intact, and the $27K student debt is very manageable.

**Upgrade to Scenario 2** if you prefer AVP graduates completely debt-free. The $9,800 in saved Stafford interest is real money, and the $27K temporary death benefit reduction is negligible against a $200K+ policy.

**Use Scenario 4** if AVLP ends up at an expensive school — the sibling 529 transfer is a powerful tax-free move that could save $20K+ in taxes on AVLP's education.

**In all scenarios:** Brokerage principal stays at $1,000,000. No PLUS loans needed. Life insurance borrowing is far superior to PLUS at 0% vs 9.08%.
