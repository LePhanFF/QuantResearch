# AVP College Financing — Deplete 529, Interest-Only Brokerage, Minimize Borrowing

**Prepared:** March 27, 2026
**Student:** AVP — UC Riverside, In-State, Fall 2026 Start
**Strategy:** Fully deplete 529 plan ($85,000); use only Treasury interest from brokerage (preserve $1M principal); minimize all external borrowing.

---

## 1. Constraints & Assumptions

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|

| Annual COA inflation | 3% |

| Brokerage account | $1,000,000 (principal untouched) |

## 8. Recommendation

**Scenario 1 is the clear winner.** The math is elegant:

- 529 ($85,000) + Treasury interest ($109,200) = $194,200
- Total cost = $196,630
- **Gap = $2,430** — filled by a trivial 0% life insurance loan
- **Zero student debt. Zero interest cost. Zero brokerage principal used. 529 fully depleted.**

**Consider Scenario 3** if you value AVP building credit history — take Stafford for 4 years, then pay it off at graduation with a 0% life insurance loan. Best of both worlds: credit building + zero net interest.

**In all scenarios:** Brokerage principal stays at $1,000,000. No PLUS loans. Life insurance is the superior gap-fill at 0%.
