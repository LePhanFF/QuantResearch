## 7. Recommendation

**Scenario 1 is recommended** if you want clean separation — AVP funded from Treasury interest + 529, AVLP funded from 529 + Stafford + life insurance. Zero brokerage principal touched, both 529 plans fully depleted.

**Upgrade to Scenario 2** if you want AVLP debt-free too — costs $27K more in life insurance borrowing but saves $9,800 in Stafford interest and your child starts adult life with zero debt.

**Use Scenario 3** if minimizing life insurance borrowing is the priority — only $5,460 borrowed, but requires splitting Treasury interest between both students (slightly more complex administration).
