# AVLP College Financing — Public University, Fall 2030 Start

**Prepared:** March 27, 2026
**Student:** AVLP — Public University (UC-level), In-State, **Fall 2030 Start**
**Strategy:** Fully deplete AVLP's 529 plan; minimize brokerage usage; leverage 4 years of 529 compound growth before enrollment.

---

## 1. Timeline Advantage

AVLP starts college **4 years after AVP** (Fall 2030 vs. Fall 2026). This creates major strategic benefits:

1. **529 compounds for 4 more years** before first withdrawal
2. **AVP finishes college (Spring 2030)** before AVLP starts — Treasury interest is **fully freed up**
3. **AVP's 529 surplus can be transferred early** and compound inside AVLP's 529
4. **Life insurance loans from AVP's plan (if any) can be repaid** before AVLP needs funds

## 10. Recommendation

**Scenario 1 is strongly recommended.** The staggered timeline fundamentally changes the math:

- AVLP's 529 grows to **$143,628** (up from $109,570 in the old simultaneous model)
- Full Treasury interest of **$27,300/yr** is available (vs. $0 when split with AVP)
- **No loans, no life insurance, no PLUS** — AVLP is fully funded from 529 + Treasury interest alone
- Even a **20% market crash** (Scenario 5) doesn't require life insurance

The only decision is whether to fully deplete the 529 (Scenario 2, adds $27K Stafford but zeroes out 529) or preserve $31K in surplus for Roth IRA rollover / grad school (Scenario 1).
