**Prepared:** April 2026
**Student:** AVLP — Public University (UC-level), In-State, **Fall 2030 Start**
**Strategy:** Deplete 529 first (5–7% growth); preserve life insurance (8–10% growth); Treasury interest fills gaps. Same growth-aware principle as AVP.

---

## 1. Timeline Advantage

AVLP starts college **4 years after AVP** (Fall 2030 vs. Fall 2026). This creates major strategic benefits:

1. **529 compounds for 4 more years** at 5–7% before first withdrawal
2. **AVP finishes college (Spring 2030)** before AVLP starts — Treasury interest is **fully freed up**
3. **AVP's 529 is fully depleted** (per revised avp.md) — no surplus transfer available
4. **Life insurance grows untouched** at 8–10% during AVP's college → ~$272K–$293K by 2030
5. **Same principle as AVP: deplete 529 (slower growth) first; preserve LI (faster growth)**
